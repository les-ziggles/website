<Timeline>
<TimelineEntry date="December 2002" title="First throws in Vaugrenier">

Fabien Hermay had discovered Ultimate in the United States and thought it would be fun to play around Nice. The first time, he and a few friends were four throwing discs. Word of mouth and friends of friends then grew the group to about fifteen people playing Ultimate once a week in the Vaugrenier park.

Not every rule of Ultimate was applied, but never mind: the fun of playing was the same. A few players in this group of friends had played Ultimate before, including Beun, who had played in Milan, and Seb, who had played with the Monkey Foo in Grenoble.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="Summer 2003" title="Julie and Martin look for a club">

Martin and Julie Pourbaix moved to Nice in 2003 and looked for an Ultimate club to keep playing the sport they had played in the north of France, then in Paris. On the website of the Fédération Flying Disc France they found that clubs had been set up in the region, in Cannes in particular, but sadly no longer existed, having failed to grow.

Keen to keep playing their favourite sport, Julie and Martin decided that the best way to find a club was to start one…

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="September 2003" title="The club is founded">

The club “Frisbee Ultimate Nice”, or FUN, was officially created, with Julie as president and Martin as secretary and treasurer. Next they had to find more players and do some promotion to bring people to training.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="12 December 2003" title="First training session">

After getting a slot on a dirt pitch from the city of Nice, the first training session took place: five people turned up! Among them was Guillaume Rame, who had just moved to Cannes and had played Ultimate in Clermont-Ferrand the year before.

The following sessions also brought David Glaizal, who used to play with the Monkey Foo and was delighted to find an Ultimate club in Nice just after moving to the region.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="Meanwhile" title="Vaugrenier finds the Nice club">

Meanwhile, the Vaugrenier group kept playing quietly every week, until Sébastien Rousset read in Infodisque-net that an Ultimate club had just started in Nice. Little by little, Vaugrenier players came to train in Nice and, the other way round, players from the Nice club came to play in Vaugrenier, bringing a few Ultimate rules with them.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="Soon after" title="The Ziggles Ultimate Club is born">

As the club grew, a general meeting was called to elect a new board and find a logo, a new name and more. Julie Pourbaix stayed president and Martin Pourbaix secretary, Philippe Teixeira was elected treasurer and Neven Allanic vice-president. Shortly after this general meeting, the club was named “Ziggles Ultimate Club” and found its logo.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="March 2004" title="Meeting the BULF">

The BULF club in Fuveau, near Aix-en-Provence, was founded at about the same time as the Ziggles Ultimate Club. The BULF invited the Ziggles to play together one Saturday, as a kind of Provence-Alpes-Côte d’Azur Ultimate championship.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="Summer 2004" title="First tournaments">

The members were so keen to play that they decided to enter tournaments, to play with other teams, learn and have fun. The first was “Tolosa 2004”, where nine players represented the club: no win, but the first prize for spirit of the game, which was of course motivating for what came next.

Still with a small squad of seven, the Ziggles Ultimate Club then went to the “Vinyl’Tour 2004”, where it won its first match in official competition.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="2004-2005 season" title="First French championships">

Barely a year old, the Ziggles Ultimate Club entered the French championships, both indoor and outdoor.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineGap />
<TimelineEntry date="9–10 September 2023" title="Hosting the Tournice">

A tournament organised in Antibes to celebrate the club’s 20th anniversary.

</TimelineEntry>
</Timeline>
