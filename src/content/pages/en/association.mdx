Our club is a non-profit association under the French law of 1901. Its missions are to:

- promote Ultimate frisbee and Disc Golf, for leisure and competition, in the Alpes-Maritimes;
- open the club to every adult and teenager from age 13, regardless of their sex, religion, disability, sexual orientation or ethnicity;
- offer volunteer-led training sessions at several levels to our members and represent the club in the national championships run by the French Flying Disc Federation.

The association’s registered office and RNA number are listed in the [legal notice](/en/legal-notice/).
