## What is Disc Golf?

It is traditional golf, only better… because you play it with discs! The rules stay the same: from a tee, reach a target, here a basket, in as few throws as possible. After each throw, the player starts again from where the disc landed.

Courses have 9 or 18 baskets. At each “hole”, your number of throws is compared with par, usually 3, 4 or 5 depending on the distance and the vegetation.

As for equipment, baskets have chains that stop the disc and drop it into the basket below. Each player builds a bag of several discs, from the putter for short distances (about twenty metres) to the driver, which can fly more than a hundred metres.

## Our sessions

The section has no fixed slot: members organise their sessions among themselves, on dates and at places that vary.

- **Where:** for example in the Valmasque forest in Antibes, at the Vaugrenier park with the portable basket, or on the courses of the region such as Figanières and Charleval.
- **On the programme:** technique (putts, drives, approaches…), a few themed holes or a full round, as the group likes.

Any section member can suggest a session and use the club’s equipment.
