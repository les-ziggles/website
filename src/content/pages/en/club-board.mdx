<TeamGrid>
<TeamMember role="President" name="JeanFi">

Captain of the ship, JeanFi sets the club’s course, represents the Ziggles with the city, the federation and our partners, and makes sure everyone finds their place, on the field and off it.

</TeamMember>
<TeamMember role="Vice-presidents" name="Marion and Kevin">

The president’s right hands, Marion and Kevin support JeanFi day to day, step in whenever needed and lend a hand wherever the club needs one.

</TeamMember>
<TeamMember role="Secretary" name="Carmela">

Meeting notices, minutes, licences and letters: Carmela keeps the club’s records. Thanks to this behind-the-scenes work, the association stays in good order.

</TeamMember>
<TeamMember role="Treasurer" name="Paul">

Membership fees, budget, grants and championship costs: Paul looks after the accounts so that every euro goes into getting the Ziggles playing… and onto the roads of France.

</TeamMember>
<TeamMember role="Disc Golf lead" name="Ben">

Ben runs the Disc Golf section: the club’s equipment, sessions among members and trips to the courses of the region. Want to throw your first discs? Ben is the person to ask.

</TeamMember>
<TeamMember role="Website lead" name="Baptiste">

Pages, the training calendar and club news: Baptiste looks after this website. Spotted something to fix or have an idea? Feel free to pass it on.

</TeamMember>
</TeamGrid>
