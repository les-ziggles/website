<TimelineEntry date="2025-2026 season" title="Consolidation in Nationale 3 and Disc Golf titles">

- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 1 (Z1, Nationale 3)**: **3rd out of 8** (top pool) and safety secured in N3, with 5 wins in 9 games and a renewed squad including 3 youth players for the first time.
- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 2 (Z2, Régionale 1)**: **6th out of 8** and safety secured in DR1 after a reprieve the previous season, with a 13-player development squad featuring 6 youth players.
- **Outdoor Ultimate – Nationale 3**: **6th** and safety secured in the club’s return to N3 (3 wins, 4 losses), with youth players fully integrated into the senior squad.
- **Disc Golf**: Mart and Ben finished **9th** in the MP40 championship. At the Ligue Sud competition, Ben (team 1) and Mart (team 2) both won the **French championship title**.

Three youth players from the club also attended the French national team tryouts.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="2024-2025 season" title="Outdoor champions and promotion to Nationale 3">

- **Outdoor Ultimate – Division Régionale**: **1st – tournament winners** and **promotion to Nationale 3**, backed by a deep squad (16 players in the first phase, 14 in the second) strengthened by youth players.
- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 1 (Z1, Nationale 2)**: competed in Nationale 2 and was relegated to Nationale 3.
- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 2 (Z2, Régionale 1)**: relegated to Division Régionale 2 (later reinstated to DR1), in a season focused on bringing youth players into indoor competition (5 in the first phase, 4 in the second).
- **Disc Golf**: **10th place** at the French Team Championships; Ben finished **9th** in Master (out of 32 qualifiers) at the French Individual Championships. On the Mistral Tour, Ben placed **5th** in Open and Julien **3rd** in Master in his very first tournament; Mart also competed on the National Tour.

</TimelineEntry>
<TimelineEntry date="2023-2024 season" title="Top pool in Nationale 2 Indoor">

- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 1 (Z1, Nationale 2)**: invited back into Nationale 2, qualified for the **top pool** and secured **N2 status**.
- **Indoor Ultimate – Team 2 (Z2, Régionale 1)**: strong campaign and **safety secured in DR1**.
- **Outdoor Ultimate – Division Régionale**: **maintained Division Régionale (DR) status**, falling just short of promotion to N3 with a 12-player bench for the final matches.
- **Disc Golf**: podium finish for Mart on the **National Tour** (**2nd** in Amateur) and qualification for the **French Individual Championships**; three members also competed regionally in Salon-de-Provence and Charleval.

</TimelineEntry>
