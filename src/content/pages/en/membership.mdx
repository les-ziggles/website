As a Ziggles member, you can come to all of our [training sessions](/en/trainings/) throughout the year and join us at the many tournaments on offer.

Membership includes the licence of the French Flying Disc Federation (<a href={SITE.federation.url}>FFFD</a>), which insures you if anything goes wrong at practice. With a competition licence, you can even represent the club in the indoor and outdoor championships!

## Prices for 2026-2027

<PriceList />

- Prices include the compulsory insurance (€4.50) and the club contribution.
- An additional IA Sport+ insurance can be added when you register, for €11.34.
- Students (18 and over) and jobseekers, with proof: **€20 off**.

## What you need

If you are new to the club, have ready:

- an ID photo;
- a <a href={SITE.medicalCertificateTemplateUrl}>medical certificate</a> made out **using the club’s template**: the federation rejects any other format.

<Callout tone="important" title="Registration happens online">
Since September 2024, you register directly on the federation’s website. Every step is explained in our [online registration guide](/en/online-registration/).
</Callout>

## Pass’Sport

The club is a Pass’Sport partner: if you are eligible, your licence price drops by €50 straight away, with nothing to pay upfront.

**Who is eligible this year?**

- young people aged 6 to 17 whose CAF or MSA family quotient is €699 or less;
- young people and adults with disabilities aged 6 to 30 who receive the AEEH or AAH allowance;
- students under 28 with a means-tested grant.

**When?** The campaign runs from 1 September to 31 December 2026. Remember to give us your Pass’Sport code when you register.

Full details (in French) on [sports.gouv.fr](https://sports.gouv.fr/pratiques-sportives/sports-pour-tous/pass-sport/) and [pass.sports.gouv.fr](https://www.pass.sports.gouv.fr/).

## Joining during the season

Want to join mid-season? Registration fees go down with the number of terms already played:

- joining in January: €10 off Ultimate, €5 off Disc Golf;
- joining in April: €20 off Ultimate, €8 off Disc Golf.
