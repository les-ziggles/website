## Ultimate in one minute

Ultimate is played with a flying disc, seven against seven outdoors and five a side indoors: you score by catching the disc in the opponents’ end zone. Mixed and non-contact, it has no referee: players call their own fouls, in the Spirit of the Game.

Our veterans explain the basics in this “collector” clip, broadcast in {SITE.introVideo.year} on the French TV channel M6 (in French).
